Questions

Distribution company finder FAQ

Why does Istanbul require a district?

Istanbul is split between BEDAŞ on the European side and AYEDAŞ on the Asian side, so province selection alone is not enough.

Can I search by hotel or street name?

This first English version intentionally avoids collecting exact address data. Select the province and, for Istanbul, district; then use the official company channel for detailed location information.

Is 186 available everywhere in Turkey?

186 is the electricity fault and outage short number used for distribution-network matters. The responsible company and alternative official contact channel vary by region.

What if the result looks wrong?

Use the EPDK official directory to verify the current service area and report the mismatch to ALO186 through the Turkish contact route.
