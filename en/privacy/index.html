Privacy · local processing · data minimisation

Which information does ALO186 process, and what should you not send?

A user should not need to share identity documents, an exact address, live location or an electricity account number merely to find the correct company or safe next step.

Direct answer: Core ALO186 tools process selections and calculations in the browser wherever practical. Turkish identity, passport, subscription, exact-address and live-location data are not requested unless a separate interaction clearly says otherwise. Hosting security logs and external sites follow their own policies.
Data flow

Separate three different situations

Immediate browser calculation

Province selection, decision tools and many calculators process inputs on the device and do not require an ALO186 account.

Browser-local storage

When an outage log or another tool stores a local record, the page identifies that behaviour. Browser clearing may remove it, and export remains under the user's control.

External and official services

Turkey's 112 service, EPDK, a distribution company, Amazon or another external destination applies its own privacy and cookie terms after the user opens it.

Sensitive information you should not send

Do not send a full identity document, Turkish identity number, card data, a meter photograph containing personal fields, electricity subscription number or exact address for a general question or correction. When an official application genuinely requires such data, use the verified authority channel.

Technical access and security logs

Web hosting, domain, security and network providers may process standard technical records such as IP address, time, browser type and requested URL under their operating and security policies. ALO186 does not claim direct control over every infrastructure log.

Cookies, localStorage and service workers

Core information pages do not require an advertising profile cookie. Some local tools may use localStorage for a preference or record, and a service worker may support offline access. These are not cookies and can be cleared through browser settings. This policy will be updated if embedded or external services change that position.

Privacy question, access or deletion request

An email sent to ALO186 may remain with the email provider so the request can be answered and documented. Avoid unnecessary personal data and identify the relevant message when requesting access or deletion.

Quick privacy check

  • Use the tools without identity details.
  • Enter official application data only on the official channel.
  • Mask personal fields in screenshots before sharing.
  • Clear local records on a shared device.
Do not delay an emergency for a privacy page. Move to a safe location and call 112.
