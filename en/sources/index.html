Which sources does ALO186 use to verify electricity guidance?

A search result or AI answer alone is not enough for a current emergency number, legal deadline, distribution-company contact or product-safety conclusion.

Direct answer: Use the competent authority for emergency numbers and public processes; EPDK for electricity-market and consumer information; the responsible distribution company's official channel for regional outages; the current manufacturer manual for exact product behaviour; and the applicable standards body for general technical requirements.

Verification method

Match the source type to the question

Emergency and phone number

The official emergency service and competent authority outrank a third-party directory.

Outage and distribution region

Cross-check the EPDK contact directory with the responsible distribution company's official outage or fault channel.

Law and consumer right

Use current legislation, an EPDK regulation and competent-authority explanation; do not infer a fixed deadline from an old news summary.

Product and technical installation

The exact model manual, data sheet, certificate and applicable standard outrank a merchant description.

Electricity distribution companies

Turkey has 21 electricity distribution regions. ALO186 maintains the province mapping and resolves Istanbul by district or side; phone numbers and official transaction pages should still be verified with EPDK or the current company channel.

Standards and manufacturer documents

IEC, ISO, IEEE or a national standard should not be used as a slogan without scope and edition. Where a standard is access-controlled, ALO186 limits the public claim to what can be verified through an open manufacturer manual or competent public document.

Source checklist

  • Is the publisher competent for the claim?
  • What is the document date and edition?
  • Does it apply in Turkey and to the exact model?
  • Does the visible document actually support the statement?
  • Does a newer official source conflict with it?
  • Can the user verify the final action from the source?
