How can ALO186 earn revenue from affiliate links?

Independent information remains free, while some external product links shown only after a verified need may support the platform.

Direct answer: ALO186 may earn commission from eligible purchases under the Amazon Associates programme and other disclosed affiliate relationships. A commercial link is visibly labelled as affiliate, sponsored or equivalent. Commission does not change the technical conclusion, and buying nothing remains a valid outcome.
Clear rules

When may an affiliate path open?

Confirm the real technical need first

Voltage, power, current, runtime, phase, protection class or compatibility must be understood before a product card is treated as relevant.

Label the commercial relationship

An affiliate destination must not be disguised as a neutral editorial link; visible disclosure and suitable HTML relationship attributes are used.

Re-check merchant information

Price, stock, delivery, warranty, seller and specification can change. Final information must be verified on the merchant page at the time of purchase.

Amazon relationship

ALO186 may use the Amazon Turkey Associates identifier alo186rehber-21. An eligible purchase through the relevant link may generate commission from Amazon. This does not mean Amazon endorses the technical suitability of the product or that ALO186 is the seller.

Safety and no-purchase boundary

  • No commercial CTA in 112, 186, fire, electric-shock, smoke, arcing or fallen-wire flows.
  • No recommendation to replace equipment that already meets the verified need.
  • No direct product match when essential technical data is missing.
  • Fixed installation, high-power, three-phase and protection equipment may require professional verification.
  • Commission rate or sponsored payment cannot control organic ranking.

Link and labelling standard

Affiliate links use rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" where appropriate. The external store is a separate service; order, payment, delivery, return and warranty terms belong to the merchant-user relationship.

Question about a commercial relationship

Report an unlabelled affiliate destination, unsuitable product match or suspected conflict of interest with the relevant page address.

What this means for the user

Affiliate commission is normally not a separate fee added by ALO186, but total price, delivery and terms must be checked on the merchant page.

ALO186 is not the seller. Product safety, installation suitability, order, payment, return and warranty are governed by the merchant and manufacturer terms.
