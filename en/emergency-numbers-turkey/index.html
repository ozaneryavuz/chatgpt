Emergency numbers Turkey

For an electricity or gas incident, call 112, 186 or 187?

The correct number depends on whether there is immediate danger, a fault on the public electricity distribution network, or a natural-gas emergency.

Direct answer: Call 112 for electric shock, fire, smoke, arcing, injury or a fallen wire. Call 186 for an electricity outage, street-lighting fault or distribution-line problem without immediate danger. Call 187 from a safe place for a suspected natural-gas smell or leak.
Call 112Call 186Call 187

One event may need two routes

A fallen electricity line is a distribution-network fault but may also be an immediate public emergency.

Safety order: keep away, warn others, call 112, then report the network fault through 186 from a safe place.

During the call

Give short, location-first information

Say what happened

Use simple words: electric shock, fire, smoke, sparking cable, fallen wire, power outage, street-light fault or gas smell.

Give the location

State the province, district, neighbourhood, street, building or a nearby landmark. If you are a visitor, ask hotel staff or a local person to confirm the address.

Follow the operator's instructions

Do not return to danger, touch equipment, operate a switch near a gas smell or attempt a repair unless emergency personnel confirm the area is safe.

Useful English phrase

“I need English. There is an emergency at …”

Then state what you can see or smell from a safe place and whether anyone is injured.

Never approach a fallen wire or gas-leak source. Electricity can return without warning, and a small spark can ignite gas.
Examples

Which route fits the situation?

There is smoke or a burning smell from an electrical cabinet

Move away, keep others back and call 112. Do not open the cabinet or pour water on electrical equipment.

The whole street is dark but there is no visible danger

Call 186 or use the responsible distribution company's official outage channel.

I smell natural gas

Do not operate switches, flames, phones or spark-producing devices near the smell. Leave by a safe route, move away and call 187. Call 112 first if there is fire, injury or immediate danger.

Only my apartment has no power

If there is no smoke, heat, water or visible damage and the rest of the building has power, contact the building manager or a qualified electrician. Do not repeatedly reset a breaker that trips again.

A cable is hanging low over a road

Keep people and vehicles away, call 112 because of the immediate public risk, and report the distribution fault through 186 from a safe place.

FAQ

Emergency-number questions

Is 112 free to call?

112 is Turkey's emergency number. Use it only for genuine emergencies so call-centre capacity remains available for people in danger.

Is 186 an emergency service?

186 is the electricity fault and outage route. Immediate threats to life or property should go to 112 first.

What is 187 in Turkey?

187 is the natural-gas emergency route. Move to a safe place before calling and follow the operator's instructions.

Can ALO186 transfer my call?

No. ALO186 provides information and direct dial links but does not answer, record or transfer calls.
