ALO186 makes the correct next step easier to understand during an electricity problem.

The platform helps separate an immediate emergency from a public-grid fault and from a problem limited to a property, without presenting itself as an official authority.

Direct answer: ALO186 helps people in Turkey reach safe, source-backed guidance for electric shock, fire, power outages, distribution companies, technical products and business continuity. It does not create official outage reports and does not imitate a government service.

Boundaries

What ALO186 is not

  • It is not Turkey's 112 service, EPDK, TEDAŞ, a municipality or an electricity distribution company.
  • It does not create an official outage, planned-work, compensation or equipment-damage record.
  • It cannot remotely guarantee that equipment is de-energised or provide a life-safety field intervention.
  • It does not publish an unverified restoration time, certain fault cause, fixed price, stock or warranty.
  • It does not sell organic technical ranking or safety conclusions in exchange for affiliate revenue.

Why a separate English URL family?

Tourists, foreign workers, students and residents in Turkey need indexable English pages that explain 112 and 186, identify the regional distribution company and label an official destination that is available only in Turkish. The /en/ structure keeps that experience discoverable and shareable.
