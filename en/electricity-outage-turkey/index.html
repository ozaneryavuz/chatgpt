Power outage Turkey · safety guide

Electricity outage in Turkey: what to do now.

First separate an immediate electrical emergency from a public-grid fault and from a problem limited to your home, hotel room, office or building.

Direct answer: Call 112 for electric shock, fire, smoke, visible arcing or a fallen conductor. Call 186 when the street, neighbourhood, transformer area or public lighting is affected. Use a qualified electrician when nearby properties have power and the fault is inside your premises.
Find the responsible companyCall 186Emergency: call 112

Do not approach

Keep away from: fallen or sagging wires, open street cabinets, flooded electrical rooms, smoke, sparking equipment, hot sockets and damaged panels.

Do not assume a cable is safe because the lights are off. A line may be energised again without warning.

Three-stage check

Classify the outage without touching electrical equipment

Is anyone in danger?

Electric shock, fire, smoke, arcing, a fallen wire or a damaged public cabinet belongs to the 112 emergency route. Move away first.

How large is the affected area?

From a safe place, look at common areas, neighbouring buildings and street lighting. A wider loss of supply usually indicates the distribution network.

Is the problem limited to one property?

If nearby properties and common areas have electricity, arrange a qualified electrician. Do not repeatedly reset a breaker that trips again.

What to tell 186

  • Province, district and neighbourhood
  • A safe location description
  • Approximate outage start time
  • Whether neighbours or street lights are affected
  • Any visible public-network hazard, described from a safe distance

Only share identity, subscription or exact account information through the verified official channel when it is genuinely required.

After reporting

Keep a simple outage record

Record the start and end time, the official report/reference number, affected areas and any safe photos of public conditions. Do not enter restricted areas or touch equipment to collect evidence.

For damaged appliances, preserve service reports, photographs and the distribution company's written response. Verify current application deadlines on the official company or EPDK source before submitting a claim.

Restoration estimates

ALO186 does not predict when power will return. Planned work can change, and unplanned faults depend on field conditions. Treat only the responsible distribution company's current channel as the source for a restoration estimate.

Questions

Power outage FAQ for English-speaking users

Should I call 186 when a wire is on the ground?

Move away and call 112 when the wire creates an immediate public danger. You may also report the distribution fault through 186 after reaching a safe place, but do not delay the emergency call.

What if I am staying in a hotel or rental property?

Tell reception, the property manager or the host after checking for immediate danger. A property-wide internal fault may need the building's authorised technical team, while a wider area outage belongs to 186.

Can I use a generator indoors or on a balcony?

No portable fuel generator should be used in an enclosed or poorly ventilated space. Carbon-monoxide and backfeed risks require a properly designed installation and safe outdoor placement.

Can I report the outage through ALO186?

No. ALO186 does not create official records. Use 112, 186 or the responsible distribution company's verified channel.
