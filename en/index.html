English electricity help in Turkey

Know whether to call 112, 186 or an electrician.

Use ALO186 to identify the safe next step during an electricity outage in Turkey and find the distribution company responsible for any of the country's 81 provinces.

Direct answer: Call 112 for electric shock, fire, smoke, visible arcing or a fallen wire. Call 186 for a neighbourhood outage, street-light fault or distribution-line problem. Contact a qualified electrician when only your home or business is affected.

What to do now

Three checks before you report the fault

Move away from danger

Do not approach a fallen wire, damaged cabinet, smoking socket, flooded electrical area or exposed conductor. Keep other people away.

Check the scope without touching equipment

Look only from a safe position: are common areas, street lights or neighbouring buildings also without power?

Use the matching channel

Call 112 for immediate danger, 186 for the public distribution network, or a qualified electrician for a fault limited to the property.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Can a tourist call 112 or 186 in Turkey?

Yes. Use 112 for an immediate emergency and 186 for an electricity distribution fault. State your location clearly and ask whether English-language support is available.

Should I reset the breaker during a neighbourhood outage?

Do not repeatedly operate breakers or open electrical panels. If there is burning smell, heat, smoke, water or visible damage, keep away and use the emergency route.

Can ALO186 tell me when electricity will return?

No. Restoration estimates can change and should come from the responsible distribution company or its official outage channel.
